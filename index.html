About Our Club

The UR-CE Red Cross Club is a student-led humanitarian movement dedicated to promoting health, disaster preparedness, and first aid awareness across Rukara Campus and the surrounding community. Through volunteerism and practical training, we empower students to act as immediate responders during emergencies.

Fundamental Principles

Humanity

Preventing and alleviating human suffering wherever it may be found, protecting life and health.

Impartiality

Making no discrimination based on nationality, race, religious beliefs, class, or political opinions.

Voluntary Service

A prompt voluntary relief movement not prompted in any manner by desire for financial gain.

Basic First Aid Guidelines

  • DRCAB Protocol: Check Danger, Response, Circulation, Airway, and Breathing during accidents.
  • Fainting: Place the individual in the recovery position and keep airway clear.
  • Bleeding: Apply direct pressure to the wound with a clean cloth or bandage.
  • Burns: Cool the affected area with clean running water for at least 10–15 minutes.
