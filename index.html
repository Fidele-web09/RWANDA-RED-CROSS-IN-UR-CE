About Our Club

The UR-CE Red Cross Club is a student-led humanitarian movement dedicated to promoting health, disaster preparedness, first aid awareness, volunteerism, and community service.

The club brings students together to develop practical skills, support vulnerable people, promote safety, and contribute to humanitarian activities within the University of Rwanda - College of Education and surrounding communities.

Fundamental Principles

Humanity

Preventing and alleviating human suffering wherever it may be found and protecting life and health.

Impartiality

Providing assistance without discrimination and giving priority to those with the greatest humanitarian needs.

Voluntary Service

A voluntary humanitarian movement dedicated to helping people rather than seeking financial gain.

Unity

There can be only one Red Cross or Red Crescent Society in any one country, open to all.

Basic First Aid Guidelines

  • Check Safety: Make sure the environment is safe before helping someone.
  • Check Response: Check whether the person responds when spoken to or gently stimulated.
  • Breathing: Check for normal breathing and seek emergency assistance when necessary.
  • Bleeding: Apply firm direct pressure with a clean dressing or cloth.
  • Burns: Cool a burn with clean running water and seek medical assistance when appropriate.

Emergency Awareness

In a serious emergency, seek professional medical or emergency assistance as soon as possible. Do not put yourself or the affected person in additional danger.
